# AceisOriginal — Brand tokens (source of truth)

**Canonical logo:** `aceisoriginal-site/public/brand/ace-cloud-logo.png` (photo 1 — Ace locked)  
SVG download may exist for press, but **PNG is the only cloud mark** for BrandLogo, CloudSVG, and backgrounds. Outlined fluffy powder-sky cloud · **no eyes** · gold stars · blush sparkle accents only.

**Type:** Nunito (ExtraBold headlines, SemiBold body)
